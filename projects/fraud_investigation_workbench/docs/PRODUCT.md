# Product brief

Fraud Investigation Workbench serves Head of Fraud / Financial Crime Technology Director.

The decision is: Can analysts trace linked activity and understand both alerts and false positives?

## Implemented outcome

Directed cycles, rolling transfer velocity and pass-through indicators identify review candidates. Synthetic labels measure precision and recall; human review transitions keep final case handling accountable.

## Acceptance

Inspect entity indicators and transaction/cycle evidence, including false positives and misses, before recording a simulated review.

## Scope boundaries

- No protected personal traits or automated account action
- Directed triangles only, not arbitrary cycle enumeration
- Heuristic scoring, not a trained or calibrated classifier
- Reviewer identity is simulated
